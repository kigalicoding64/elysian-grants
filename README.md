# ElScholarship — Global Academic Mobility Platform

ElScholarship is a verified global scholarship and academic mobility directory designed to bridge the opportunity gap for students and researchers. The platform aggregates thousands of fully funded university grants, research fellowships, living stipends, and international study awards with step-by-step guidance.

---

## 📌 Platform Overview

* **Official Domain:** [https://elscholarship.com](https://elscholarship.com)
* **Core Focus:** Fully Funded Scholarships, Post-Doctoral Fellowships, Undergraduate & Postgraduate Grants.
* **Target Audience:** International students, researchers, and academic applicants worldwide.
* **Key Features:** Real-time deadline tracking, verified grant breakdowns, automated summary generation for social media sharing, and managed application support.

---

## 🎯 Key Opportunity Categories

ElScholarship indexes verified opportunities across multiple degree levels and funding structures:

1. **Fully Funded University Grants:**
   * 100% tuition fee exemptions covered directly by host institutions or government bilateral agreements.
   * Full coverage for laboratory, administrative, and institutional fees.

2. **Living Allowance & Stipend Awards:**
   * Tax-exempt monthly cost-of-living stipends adjusted to local purchasing power.
   * On-campus university accommodation and meal allowances.

3. **International Mobility & Travel Support:**
   * Roundtrip economy airfare grants with baggage allowances.
   * Visa support and health insurance coverage.

4. **Research & Post-Doctoral Fellowships:**
   * Dedicated funding for post-doctoral research projects (including Data Sciences, Health Informatics, and Public Health initiatives).
